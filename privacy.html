AI Hub — Privacy policy

AI Hub accesses Google user data only for the account owner who authorised it, and only to perform actions the owner requests.

  • Data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Data is not used for advertising or for training AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and private server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
